# Code of Conduct

**Project:** EAV-TIRD-001 — Theory of Isospatial Reflexive Dimensions (TIRD)
**Organization:** Earth Angel Ventures

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex, gender identity, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

This is a research space. Critique the theory. Do not attack the person.

## Our Standards

**Examples of positive behavior:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the science
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Citing sources when you claim a fact

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith arguments
- Claiming TIRD is proven, or claiming you can sell access to dimensions
- Spamming Issues with promotions, courses, healings, or products
- Posting personal data, phone numbers, or addresses of anyone except the official contact already listed in CONTACT.md
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Mocking faith, including the verses cited in this repository

## Enforcement

**Reporting:** Email earthangelventuresph@gmail.com with subject `[CONDUCT] EAV-TIRD-001` or open a private GitHub Issue.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces: GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, emails related to the project, and any event representing the project.
