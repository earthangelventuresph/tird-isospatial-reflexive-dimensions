# Earth Angel Ventures

## 1. Identity

**Legal Name:** Earth Angel Ventures
**Short Name:** EAV
**Research Program:** Consciousness, Quantum Information, and Multidimensional Structure
**Research Standard:** The standard is not how extraordinary a claim sounds, but how reliably the evidence supports it and how well others can examine, challenge, reproduce, and build upon the work.

Earth Angel Ventures is a research-driven venture. Its work is not speculation for entertainment. It is structured theory-building with falsifiable predictions, version-controlled protocols, and public transparency.

## 2. Mission

To formalize, test, and publish frameworks that re-examine the structure of reality, perception, consciousness, and information — and to do so with the same rigor demanded of any scientific program.

Earth Angel Ventures does not claim results it does not have. It claims a clear theory, clear predictions, and a clear path to test them.

## 3. Vision

A science where:
- Dimensions are not assumed to be elsewhere. They are examined where they are claimed to be.
- Perception is not assumed to be complete. It is measured as a window.
- Consciousness is not treated as an afterthought. It is treated as a measurable variable.
- Extraordinary claims are held to ordinary standards of evidence — preregistration, replication, and falsification.

## 4. What Earth Angel Ventures Does

**1. Theoretical Research**
Development of formal models with defined symbols, equations, and operational definitions. Every term in TIRD has a definition. Every postulate has a test.

**2. Experimental Protocol Design**
Design of complete, preregistered experiments with sample-size calculations, controls, blinding, and falsification thresholds decided in advance.

**3. Open Research Publication**
Publication of manuscripts, protocols, figures, and — once collected — data and analysis code in version-controlled public repositories.

**4. Future Applied Research**
Only after empirical support is established, exploration of implications for perception science, memory research, signal science, and consciousness research. No applied claims are made before evidence.

## 5. What Earth Angel Ventures Does NOT Do

- Does not claim empirical results before data are collected.
- Does not claim inter-layer travel has been demonstrated.
- Does not claim parallel Earths exist.
- Does not claim ethics approval, funding, or partnerships that do not exist.
- Does not fabricate data, citations, or affiliations.
- Does not sell access to dimensions, portals, or consciousness expansion.
- Does not offer medical, therapeutic, or spiritual services based on TIRD.

If it is not in this repository with a version number and a date, Earth Angel Ventures does not claim it.

## 7. Governance and Accountability

**Research Owner:** Earth Angel Ventures
**Principal Investigator:** Angelica Pangan
**Decision Authority:** The Principal Investigator holds final authority over scientific content, protocol design, and publication decisions.

**Core Governance Rules:**
1. No manuscript is published without a version number, date, and status label.
2. No protocol is executed without preregistration and a frozen analysis script.
3. No data are reported without a data-availability statement matching actual arrangements.
4. No post-hoc analysis is presented as if it were pre-registered.
5. All deviations from a protocol are listed publicly.
6. All authors are responsible for verifying equations, claims, and citations before submission.

## 8. Transparency Commitments

Earth Angel Ventures commits to:
- Public version control for all manuscripts and protocols
- Public falsification thresholds before data collection
- Public reporting of null and negative results
- Public listing of protocol deviations
- Public correction of errors via versioned updates, not silent edits
- Accurate authorship, funding, and competing-interest statements
