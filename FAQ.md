## 1. Is TIRD proven?
No. TIRD is a hypothesis and experimental protocol document v1.0. No empirical data have been collected yet. No results are claimed.

## 2. Is this parallel Earths / multiverse?
No. TIRD rejects parallel Earths. Layers coexist at the SAME spacetime coordinate, distinguished by frequency band, not by location.

## 3. Is this branching timelines / Many-Worlds?
No. TIRD rejects branching timelines. Layers are simultaneous and continuously coupled, not sequential after measurement.

## 4. Can I travel between dimensions?

## 7. What is isospatial?
Isospatial means "same place." Multiple layers occupy the identical spacetime support. They are not elsewhere. They are here, at the same coordinates, separated by frequency.

## 8. What is the perception window?
An observer has a native frequency omega_O and bandwidth sigma_O. The coupling kernel G is Gaussian. Layers far from your native frequency have G near zero and are invisible to you. A portal is not a doorway. It is a resonance condition where G crosses a threshold.
