- Personal expenses unrelated to research

## How Funders Are Acknowledged

- Name / organization listed in CHANGELOG.md and in manuscript acknowledgements
- Funder name listed in LEGAL-ETHICS.md Funding section
- Funder receives pre-publication summary of results (positive or null)

**Funding does NOT buy:**
- Authorship (unless funder contributes intellectually and meets authorship criteria)
- Control over results
- Right to suppress null results
- Right to change falsification thresholds after data collection

## Funding Principles

1. **Independence:** Results are published regardless of outcome.
2. **Transparency:** All funding amounts, sources, and uses are listed publicly.
3. **No strings:** No funder can dictate conclusions.
4. **Ethics first:** No human work without ethics approval.
5. **Open data:** Data and code published openly per consent.
