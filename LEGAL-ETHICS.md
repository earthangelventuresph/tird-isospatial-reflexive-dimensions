### 9. Core Ethical Principles

This research follows these principles, without exception:

**1. Respect for Persons**
Participants are autonomous. Participation is voluntary. Withdrawal is allowed at any time, without penalty, without need to give a reason.

**2. Beneficence**
Risks are minimized. Benefits are not overstated. No participant is exposed to risk for the sake of a theoretical claim.

**3. Justice**
Participant selection is fair. No group is exploited. No group is excluded without scientific justification.

**4. Integrity**
No fabrication. No falsification. No selective reporting. No hidden post-hoc analysis presented as pre-registered.

**5. Transparency**
Methods, thresholds, deviations, and null results are published.

### 10. Informed Consent - Required Elements

For every human-participant protocol, informed consent must include:

1. Purpose of the study in plain language
2. What the participant will be asked to do
3. Duration and number of sessions
4. Risks and discomforts, including fatigue, eye strain, boredom, and time burden
5. That there is no direct benefit to the participant beyond contribution to research
6. That participation is voluntary and withdrawal is allowed at any time
7. How data will be stored, pseudonymized, and who will have access
8. How long data will be retained
9. Contact for questions and for complaints
10. That TIRD is a hypothesis under test, not a proven theory or treatment

Consent must be obtained in writing (or approved electronic equivalent) before any data collection. Verbal consent alone is not sufficient unless approved by the ethics board.

**Special rule for TIRD:** Participants must NOT be told that the study will give them access to other dimensions, improve psychic abilities, or prove parallel realities. The study tests memory, perception, and synchrony. That is what consent describes.

### 11. Participant Protection

- **Confidentiality:** Names are replaced with participant codes at collection. The code key is stored separately with restricted access.
- **Pseudonymization:** All datasets for analysis use codes, not names.
- **Data Minimization:** Only data specified in the protocol are collected. No extra personal data.
- **Storage:** Data stored with restricted access, encrypted where required by institutional policy. Retention period to be specified in each approved protocol.
- **Withdrawal:** If a participant withdraws, their data are handled per the approved protocol and their stated preference (retain anonymized data vs. delete). Withdrawal does not affect any other rights.
- **Vulnerable Participants:** No protocol in this program targets children, persons with cognitive impairment, or other vulnerable groups without additional ethics safeguards and specific approval. As of v1.0, all human protocols are adult-only.

### 12. Risk Assessment

**Physical Risks:**
- E1, E3, E6: Laser exposure, EM exposure. Mitigation: Laser safety review, protective equipment, exposure limits, trained operator only.
- E4, E7, E8, E9: Fatigue, eye strain, headache, boredom. Mitigation: Session limits, breaks, stopping rules, voluntary withdrawal.

**Psychological Risks:**
- Frustration from difficult tasks, performance anxiety. Mitigation: Clear instructions, reassurance that performance is not a measure of worth, debrief.
- Misinterpretation: Participant may believe study proves dimensions exist. Mitigation: Debrief explicitly states hypothesis status and that no results are claimed.

**Social Risks:**
- Breach of confidentiality. Mitigation: Pseudonymization, restricted access, no publication of identifiable data.

**No protocol involves:** Deception about risks, physical harm, invasive procedures, drug administration, or withholding of safety information.
