## ABSTRACT

Standard many-worlds interpretations and multiverse models treat alternative realities as either branching timelines that separate after measurement events or as spatially distant parallel universes located on different Earths. The Theory of Isospatial Reflexive Dimensions rejects both pictures.

TIRD asserts that multiple frequency-stratified layers of reality coexist as reflexive mirror states inside one and the same spacetime coordinate. They occupy a single shared quantum space. Perception is restricted to the vibrational band with which an observer's sensory and cognitive apparatus resonates. Higher-frequency observers can couple into lower bands, but sustained presence incurs a measurable dwell-time cost.

The defining geometric claims of TIRD are therefore isospatial coexistence and reflexive symmetry under the palindromic operator that realizes the sequence **1-2-3-3-2-1**.

**Objective.** To formalize TIRD as a mathematically explicit model with operational definitions for every term, to derive quantitative predictions that distinguish the model from standard quantum mechanics and standard wave-propagation theory, and to provide complete experimental protocols capable of confirming or falsifying each core postulate.

**Methods.** The theory is constructed from ten primary postulates and six extended mechanisms. These are expressed through a layered Hilbert space in which every layer occupies the identical spacetime support, a Gaussian perception kernel, a nonlinear dwell-cost functional, a palindromic mirror operator, observer-relative time offsets, a generalized effective-velocity law that includes information content, inter-layer entanglement couplings, an integrated-information consciousness measure, and conservation across matter, antimatter, dark-matter, and field sectors.

**Results.** None. This is a theoretical and protocol paper. All statements are hypotheses or derivations from the postulates. No empirical findings are reported or implied.

**Interpretation.** If the model is correct, measurable dependencies will appear in four primary observables and five extended observables. Absence of those dependencies rejects the corresponding postulates. Because the postulates are designed to be separable, failure of one does not collapse the entire framework.

**Conclusion.** TIRD is offered as a falsifiable framework. Its scientific status will be decided solely by the outcomes of the specified experiments.

**Keywords:** Isospatial coexistence, reflexive dimensions, frequency stratification, shared quantum space, entanglement, palindromic symmetry, observer-relative time, consciousness, memory integration, signal propagation, push-pull polarity, on-off gating, curvature, frame recurrence.

## THE FORMAL MODEL

### P1. Isospatial Layered Coexistence
Let Q be a single quantum space with Hilbert space H = direct sum of H_k for k = 1 to N. Each layer H_k occupies the identical spacetime support and is characterized by frequency band B_k. Layers are simultaneous components of one state. They are not separate timelines. They are not located on different Earths. They share every spacetime coordinate.

### P2. Perception Window
An observer O possesses native frequency omega_O and bandwidth sigma_O. The coupling kernel G is Gaussian: exp[-(omega_k - omega_O)^2 / 2 sigma_O^2]. Perceived reality is the normalized projection of the total state through this kernel. Layers with G near zero are invisible. A portal occurs when G crosses a detection threshold. The portal is not a spatial doorway. It is a resonance condition at the same coordinates.

### P3. Dwell-Time Cost of Presence
An observer with omega_O > omega_k may couple into layer k. Presence perturbs the layer state. Cumulative disturbance D is the time integral of Gamma. Cost C = C_0 * (e^(tau / tau_c) - 1). Stability requires D <= D_crit.

### P4. Reflexive Palindromic Symmetry
Define the sequence S = (s1, s2, s3, s3, s2, s1) and the mirror operator R that reverses index order. Then R S = S. Scale self-similarity holds up to bounded error. Molecular chirality is represented by the parity operator that maps left-handed to right-handed states.

### P5. Observer-Relative Time
Time is treated as an index on the shared manifold, not a flow. Each observer carries an offset theta_O. For observers A and B: t_A = t_B + kappa * (omega_A - omega_B). An event fixed in the block can lie in one observer's past, another's present, and a third's future. Time travel in this model is a change of slice, a shift of theta_O, not an alteration of a flowing timeline.

### P6. Generalized Propagation Law
Effective velocity = c / (n + eta_obstruction + eta_position + eta_information). The information term is the Shannon entropy of the transmitted stream. Vacuum c remains constant.

### P7. Entanglement as Inter-Layer Coupling
Subsystems in different layers share an entangled singlet-like state. Cross-layer coupling strength = J_0 * G * exp(-D / D_crit). The CHSH parameter is the standard correlator combination, bounded by 2 for local theories and by 2*sqrt(2) for quantum mechanics.

### P8. Consciousness Measure
Every system carries a nonnegative integrated-information value Phi. The falsifiable claim is that Phi correlates with the system perception-window width according to a linear relation with positive slope.

### P9. Memory Integration
Memory integration of an encoded sequence is the weighted sum of inner products between elements and their positional mirrors. The sum is maximal when the sequence is palindromic. Palindromic encodings produce the highest integration scores.

### P10. Conservation Across Sectors
Total energy content is conserved across all layers and across the four sectors of matter, antimatter, dark matter, and field energy. Dark matter is modeled as the matter content of layers with near-zero perception kernel relative to ordinary observers.

### Extended Mechanisms P11-P16
**P11 Push-Pull Polarity:** Layer occupation obeys a rate equation driven by push-pull difference. Ratio = 1 at balance.
**P12 On-Off Gate:** Coupling is active only above a threshold. Hysteresis width is the measurable variable.
**P13 Curvature:** Concave and convex local geometry are distinct dimensional aspects of the same spacetime support.
**P14 Frame Recurrence:** The reflexive sequence implies periodic return of frames.
**P15 Intuition Accuracy and Delay:** Intuitive judgment is a rapid low-bandwidth readout from a broader perception window.
**P16 Projection and Alignment:** Projection maps internal state onto external layer. Alignment is match under the reflexive operator.
