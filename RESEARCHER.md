## 2. Research Position

The position is stated with conviction and with discipline:

**What is asserted:**
- That isospatial coexistence is a coherent, formalizable alternative to branching timelines and parallel Earths.
- That reflexive symmetry under the palindromic operator 1-2-3-3-2-1 provides a unifying structure for push-pull, on-off, left-right, and concave-convex phenomena.
- That perception can be modeled as a Gaussian window on frequency, not as a complete view of reality.
- That presence in a non-native layer carries a measurable, nonlinear dwell-time cost.
- That these claims are falsifiable through nine specified experiments.

**What is NOT asserted:**
- That any experiment has been performed.
- That any result has been obtained.
- That inter-layer travel has been demonstrated.
- That consciousness has been proven to be quantum.
- That dark matter has been proven to be layer content.

Conviction in the theory does not replace evidence. It drives the demand for evidence.

## 3. Research Approach

Angelica Pangan works from a single standard:

> The standard is not how extraordinary a claim sounds, but how reliably the evidence supports it and how well others can examine, challenge, reproduce, and build upon the work.

This means:
1. **Formalize first.** No term is left vague. If it cannot be defined, it cannot be tested.
2. **Predict, then test.** Every postulate is converted into a quantitative prediction before data are collected.
3. **Falsify on purpose.** Every protocol carries a threshold that would reject the postulate. A theory that cannot fail is not a theory.
4. **Separate the claims.** P1 through P16 are separable. Failure of one does not collapse the whole framework, and success of one does not prove the whole framework.
5. **Publish openly.** Version control, open protocols, and public corrections.
